Environmental Pledge

100% Eco-Friendly Lead-Acid Recycling

Automotive batteries contain hazardous lead and sulfuric acid. IonCo maintains a strict zero-dumping policy. Every old core battery removed during our doorstep swaps is safely neutralized and processed through government-certified recycling facilities.
