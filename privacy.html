Data Sovereignty & Protection

Privacy Policy

At IonCo Pro, we are firmly committed to safeguarding your personal information, vehicle telemetry, and transaction history with enterprise-grade encryption.

Last Updated: June 2026 • Effective Worldwide

1. Information We Collect

When you utilize our doorstep roadside assistance, book battery replacements, or configure fleet SaaS telemetry workspaces, we collect specific data necessary to deliver seamless service. This includes your full name, registered mobile phone number, vehicle make and model, precise GPS dispatch landmarks, and digital payment receipts.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your information is utilized solely to dispatch emergency mobile workshop vans, verify battery warranty validity, process secure UPI and card payments, and send automated voltage telemetry drop warnings to your registered device. We never sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third-party marketing brokers.

3. Encryption & Data Security

All communications between your client dashboard and our cloud servers are protected via end-to-end AES-256 encryption and TLS 1.3 transfer protocols. Sensitive database parameters are tokenized, ensuring compliance with global data protection frameworks and regional safety standards.

4. Data Purge & Your Rights

You maintain complete sovereignty over your records. Through your customer operations dashboard, you can request an immediate GDPR data purge to permanently scrub all historical vehicle telemetry, session logs, and contact details from our active databases within 72 hours.

5. Contact Our Privacy Officer

If you have any questions, compliance concerns, or formal data requests regarding this Privacy Policy, please reach out directly to our security command desk at dispatch@IonCo.in.
