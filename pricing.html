Core Recycling Program

How Old Battery Core Exchange Works

Under environmental laws and industry standards, vehicle batteries are priced with an exchange model. When our technician delivers your brand-new battery, they take your old dead battery away. This grants you an instant discount of up to ₹1,500 on your invoice while ensuring hazardous lead and acid are safely recycled.
